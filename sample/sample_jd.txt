Senior Backend Engineer — Cloud Platform Team

About the Role:
We are looking for a Senior Backend Engineer to join our Cloud Platform team. You will be
responsible for designing, building, and maintaining scalable distributed systems that power
our core product infrastructure.

Responsibilities:
- Design and implement high-throughput, low-latency backend services using Python and Go.
- Lead the architecture of microservices running on AWS (ECS, Lambda, RDS, S3).
- Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines using GitHub Actions and Terraform.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams (Product, DevOps, Frontend) to define APIs.
- Mentor junior engineers and conduct thorough code reviews.
- Drive observability best practices: metrics, logging, and distributed tracing (Datadog).
- Participate in on-call rotations and incident response.

Requirements:
- 5+ years of backend engineering experience.
- Proficiency in Python; experience with FastAPI or Django.
- Strong experience with AWS cloud services (EC2, Lambda, RDS, S3, SQS, ECS).
- Experience with containerisation (Docker, Kubernetes).
- Solid understanding of distributed systems, message queues (Kafka, SQS), and databases (PostgreSQL).
- Experience with Infrastructure as Code (Terraform or CDK).
- Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines and DevOps practices.
- Excellent communication skills and ability to work in a fast-paced environment.

Nice to Have:
- Experience with Go or Rust.
- Knowledge of data streaming (Kafka, Kinesis).
- Contributions to open-source projects.

What We Offer:
- Competitive salary and equity package.
- Remote-first culture with flexible working hours.
- Annual learning & development budget.
- Health, dental, and vision insurance.
